How we check a deal

  1. Find it in a source that names the store. Every price here comes from the store’s own listing as reported by an independent deals roundup, never from a guess.
  2. Do the maths. The discount on each card is computed from the two prices shown. If they don’t add up, the deal doesn’t ship.
  3. Judge the “was” price. A strikethrough that was never a selling price is flagged, not celebrated. Two TV deals below carry that warning.
  4. Call it honestly. New low means the lowest price reported yet. Strong deal is a deep cut on a real selling price. Good deal is worth it if you wanted the item. Modest is a small saving. Check the reference means the reference price is inflated.
